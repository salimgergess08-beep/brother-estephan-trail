BEFORE THE LEBANESE POUND

A currency did not appear overnight.

Before 1918

Ottoman money

The Ottoman pound circulated in Lebanon while it was part of the Ottoman Empire.

1918–1920

A short transition

Egyptian currency, linked to sterling, followed Ottoman rule before the French monetary system took over.

1920s–1940s

Toward a national lira

Mandate-era money was linked to France. Independence in 1943 and the 1948 monetary agreement marked steps toward monetary autonomy.

03 / THE QUESTION TO TAKE HOME

Is a bigger number
always worth more?

Face value is printed on the note. Purchasing power is what that note lets you buy. Lebanon’s monetary history shows why the two are different.

Reveal the takeaway

A LL100,000 note can buy less in one period than a much smaller note did in another. Compare prices, wages and dates—not just the zeros.
